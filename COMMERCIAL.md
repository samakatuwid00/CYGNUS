# Who may use Cygnus for free, and who needs a licence

Cygnus is licensed under the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE.md).
In short - the licence itself is what counts:

## Free

- **Students**, for study, school work and personal projects.
- **Schools and universities**, and other educational institutions, public
  research organizations, charities and government institutions, whatever
  their funding.
- **Anyone**, for personal use with no commercial application: learning,
  testing, a hobby.

## Needs a commercial licence

- A company or business using Cygnus in its work.
- Anyone using Cygnus to earn money: in paid work for clients, or as part
  of a product or service that is sold.
- Selling, reselling or bundling Cygnus, changed or not.

Not sure? Ask before you use it commercially; see below.

## Contributing

Contributions are welcome under an agreement that lets the author offer
them under both the noncommercial licence and the commercial one; without
it, a change cannot be taken in. Ask for the agreement before you send one.

## Other people's software inside Cygnus

The installer carries other projects' software under their own licences -
MIT, BSD, Apache 2.0, the Python licence, LGPL for edge-tts, MPL for
certifi. They keep those licences; this one does not change them. The list
is in [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).
